## Pink Bunny review lesson

The recorded test used a bead-rolling opener, palm and wearing shots, English VO and matching captions. The review identified slow altered VO, missing audible music, light-background caption contrast, a separate promotional overlay, and slow later display shots. For similar revisions, verify audible music both on its own track and during playback, restore original-speed narration, make the captions single-line and high enough for Reels, keep the first roll uninterrupted and tighten the following cuts. Treat the old suggestion of about four seconds per later shot as feedback on that test. Start around three seconds under the current guide. Inspect the timeline at the user's requested review point before export.
